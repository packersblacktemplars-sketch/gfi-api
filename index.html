Two hosts, same bytes. This page and every route above serve application/json. The branded base on Hugging Face is the one to cite; that host serves every JSON file as text/plain, so point a strict client at this host instead.

Series id grammar

GEO.FAMILY.MEASURE, uppercase, dots, issued once and never reused. GEO is ISO3. Families are POLICY (RATE, REAL), PRICE (CPI_YOY), FISCAL (DEBT_GDP), MACRO (GDP_GROWTH), DEMO (six population and labour measures) and, on the licensed tier, RULE (eight statutory limits).

Four rules the data follows

  1. A source is named only when that source carries the figure. Where it does not, the source is null and the status says unresolved. 115 of 191 series are in that state today rather than borrowing a neighbour's name.
  2. Annual figures are periods, not dates. The as-of field is null, the period is the year and an estimate flag travels with it. No figure is stamped December 31 to make a column look tidy.
  3. A derived figure publishes its inputs and a verdict on comparability. The real policy rate carries the date of the rate and the period of the price index it was deflated by. 3 series are flagged for review, including the United States, whose real rate still rests on 2024 inflation.
  4. Empty is declared. An uncollected block says so. It never renders as a blank cell or a zero.
